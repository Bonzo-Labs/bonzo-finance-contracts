Root Cause: Supra Provider Transition Failure

Supra confirmed a data provider failure during a provider transition (one going down, one onboarding). The faulty provider returned ~221× the correct STEAM/HBAR value. When both were simultaneously active, the aggregate doubled to ~456×. This is confirmed by back-calculation from the Aave V2 liquidation formula, which implies a price of exactly 15.728 HBAR — matching the CSV peak of 15.728162 HBAR at 07:20:48 UTC.

Mechanism: STEAM as Debt = Instant Health Factor Collapse

Any user who had borrowed STEAM found their debt valued 463× higher than reality. This instantly pushed health factors far below 1.0 for all 6 users, enabling liquidations even on healthy positions. WHBAR collateral (hardcoded at 10^18 in SupraOracle.sol) was priced correctly — so the entire distortion was on the debt side, making each STEAM token "worth" ~15.73 HBAR instead of ~0.034 HBAR.

Broader Damage: 415,075 WHBAR Total — Not Just 301,593

Initial analysis focused on the primary victim (0.0.2964381). Reviewing all protocol liquidation events reveals 5 additional users were hit by the same oracle failure, adding 113,482 WHBAR in wrongful seizures. The second-largest victim (0.0.847393) lost 97,734 WHBAR across 14 transactions spread over 3 hours.
